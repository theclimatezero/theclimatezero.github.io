Beyond national totals
Beyond countries

It isn't just countries. It's companies.

Every other page on this site organises emissions by country, which is how most climate data is reported. But a huge share of historical emissions traces back to a strikingly small number of specific companies and state-owned fossil fuel producers, entities with names, headquarters, and shareholders, not just national totals. That distinction matters for accountability.

1854
The start year of the Carbon Majors database, the primary independent research project tracking company-level historical fossil fuel and cement emissions. Carbon Majors
37.4 Gt
Total fossil CO2 emissions tracked by the Carbon Majors database in 2024 alone, across all producers it monitors. Carbon Majors 2024 Data Update
~57
The approximate number of entities linked to roughly 80% of global fossil fuel and cement emissions between 2016 and 2022, according to the Carbon Majors 2023 update. Carbon Majors
3 types
The database classifies every major emitter as a nation-state producer, a state-owned company, or an investor-owned company, a useful lens for who actually controls the decision to keep extracting. Carbon Majors
Why there's no ranked table here. We deliberately have not published a ranked list of specific companies with exact percentage figures on this page. When we checked the Carbon Majors database's own published rankings against multiple citations, the numbers varied between sources depending on when and how they were pulled, and we were not able to confirm a single, current, authoritative top-20 list to a standard we're comfortable publishing. Rather than guess or round to something that looks plausible, we're linking you directly to the source.

This is a deliberate editorial choice, not a gap we plan to quietly fill in later with invented numbers. If you're citing this page academically: the honest, defensible claim is the concentration finding itself (a small number of entities account for a large share of emissions), sourced to Carbon Majors directly, rather than any specific rank ClimateZero might publish secondhand.
