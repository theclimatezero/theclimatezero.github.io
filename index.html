What is actually happening

Colour is disappearing from the world, in ways you can measure.

Smoke greys out skies that were blue. Bleaching turns living reef white and grey where colour used to be. Drought drains green land to dust. None of this is a metaphor, it shows up in the data below, place by place, year by year.

SmokeGlobal fire outbreaks hit a record high in early 2026, greying skies far beyond the fire itself.
FloodingNow the foremost climate hazard worldwide, leaving grey silt where fields used to be.
EmissionsCarbon pollution reached record levels again in 2025, the source the rest of this page traces back to.
